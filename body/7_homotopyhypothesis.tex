\section{Homotopy Hypothesis} \label{sec: homotopy hypothesis}

We prove that a natural transformation of topological realization induces a natural weak equivalence between the associated Kan extensions to models.

We prove that a topological realization induces a Quillen equivalence between models over a coherator and spaces and homotopies between topological realization give rise to natural weak equivalences between the corresponding Quillen adjunctions.
