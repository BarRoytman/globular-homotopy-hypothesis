\section{Introduction} \label{sec: introduction}

Theorem 1: For any coherator, the Grothendieck--Quillen model structure on the category of models over the coherator exists. Furthermore, every topological realization of a coherator induces a Quillen equivalence between the category of spaces with the Quillen model structure and models of a coherator with the Grothendieck--Quillen model structure. Homotopies between topological realization give rise to natural weak equivalences between the corresponding Quillen adjunctions.

Theorem 2: Every cellular extension of cellular globular extensions that are equipped with standard model structures such that the right adjoint preserves cofibrations and acyclic cofibrations and unit applied to cell complexes is a weak equivalence induces a Quillen equivalence. The right adjoint preserves cofibrations in the case that the source of the cellular extension recieves a globular functor from the globular completion such that with image in the subcategory of monomorphisms and the unit is a weak equivalence whenever it is a weak equivalence on representables. In particular, every cellular extesnion of models over a coherator with Grothendieck--Quillen model structures is a Quillen equivalence.

Theorem 3: Every Ara coherator is a coherator.
