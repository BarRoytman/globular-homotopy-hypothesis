\section{Constructions in Coherators and Models}\label{sec: constructions in coheratorsandmodels}

We construct various important morphisms in a admissibly filled cellular extension with chosen admissible fillings.

We construct the first frustum cylinder functor and prove its basic properties.

We construct the barycentric subdivison functor and prove its basic properties.

In this section we should explicitly name the fillings we will use (Notation subject to change):

$i_k$ fills $(\iota_k, \iota_k)$ where $\iota_k \colon G^k \to G^{\mathbf{k}}$ is the canonical isomorphism

$c^m_{n, p}$ for $n > m <p$ fills $(\sigma_T^{n, 0}, \tau_T^{n, 1})$ when $n = p$, $(\langle \id_n, \sigma^q \rangle\circ c^m_{n, q}, \langle \id_n, \tau^q \rangle \circ c^m_{n, q})$ when $n \leq q < q+1 = p$, and $(\langle \sigma^r, \id_p \rangle\circ c^m_{r, q}, \langle \tau^r, \id_p \rangle \circ c^m_{r, q})$ when $n = r+1 > r \geq p$.

$\rho_k$ fills $(\tau_{\mathbf{k + 1}, 0}^k, \sigma^k_{\mathbf{k + 1}, 0})$.
