\section{Models over Cellular Globular Extensions}\label{sec: models over cellular globularextensions}

We name every element of the target of relative cell complexes.

We prove relative cell complexes are closed under the forgetful functor of a cellular extension of globular extension.

We prove that for a cellular extension of globular extensions such that the source recieves a faithful globular functor from the globular completion whose image is contain in the subcategory of monomorphisms, the right adjoint of the induced adjunction between categories of models preserves cofibrations and the unit is a weak equivalence on cellular obects whenever it is a weak equivalence on representables.

We prove that every cellular extension of cellular globular extensions that are equipped with standard model structures such that the right adjoint preserves cofibrations and acyclic cofibrations and unit applied to cell complexes is a weak equivalence induces a Quillen equivalence.
