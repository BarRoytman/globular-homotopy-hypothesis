\section{Introduction} \label{sec: introduction}

Every Ara coherator is a coherator.

For any coherator, the Grothendieck--Quillen model structure on the category of models over the coherator exists.

Every cellular extension of coherators induces a Quillen equivalence with respect to the corresponding Grothendieck--Quillen model structures.

Every topological realization of a coherator induces a Quillen equivalence between the category of spaces with the Quillen model structure and models of a coherator with the Grothendieck--Quillen model structure.
